Gita Saathi ("the App") is a spiritual companion for Bhagavad Gita study, japa meditation, quizzes, and AI-powered guidance. This policy explains what data the App collects, why, and how it is handled.

1. What We Collect

Data stored only on your device

The following data never leaves your device unless you explicitly create a cloud backup:

  • Japa counter totals, session history, and per-name counts
  • Quiz progress and scores
  • Bookmarked and read verses, reading progress
  • App preferences (language, theme, notification times, dark mode)
  • Streak and visit history
  • Subscription status (stored in encrypted local storage)

Data processed by third-party services

AI Chat (Groq): When you use the "Ask Krishna" feature, the question you type is sent to Groq, Inc.'s API to generate a response. Your questions are used solely to produce the answer and are not used to identify you. We do not store your questions on any server. Groq's own privacy policy governs their handling of data they receive.

Google AdMob: The App displays banner advertisements via Google AdMob. AdMob may collect your device's advertising ID and ad-interaction signals to serve and measure ads. Where required by law (e.g., GDPR/EEA), a consent prompt is shown before personalised ads are loaded. For details, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

Firebase Analytics: We use Google Firebase Analytics to understand how the App is used — for example, which screens are visited, session duration, and device type. This data is aggregated and does not personally identify you. See Google's partner policy.

Firebase Crashlytics: When the App crashes, a crash report is sent to Firebase Crashlytics. Reports include device model, OS version, stack traces, and app state at the time of the crash. No personal content (such as your japa counts or chat messages) is included.

Firebase Performance Monitoring: The App automatically collects performance metrics such as app startup time, screen rendering times, and HTTP request latency. This data helps us identify and fix performance issues. No personal content is included.

Data collected with your permission

Google Sign-In (optional): If you choose to sign in with Google, the App receives your name and email address. Your email is used to check subscription and VIP status. Sign-in is not required to use the App.

Google Drive Backup (user-initiated): If you sign in and choose to back up your data, an encrypted copy of your app data is saved to your own Google Drive in the app-specific hidden folder (appData scope). The developer cannot access this folder — only you can, via your Google account. You can delete this backup at any time.

Google Play Billing: If you purchase a subscription, the transaction is processed entirely by Google Play. The developer never receives or stores your payment card details, bank information, or billing address. We only receive confirmation of whether a subscription is active.

Microphone (voice japa): The japa counter offers an optional voice-input mode that uses your device's built-in speech recognition to detect chant repetitions. Audio is processed on your device by Android's speech recognition engine. The App itself does not record, store, or transmit audio. Microphone access is requested only when you activate voice mode and can be revoked in Android Settings at any time.

Activity Recognition (step counter): The japa counter offers an optional step-counter feature that uses your device's motion sensors (accelerometer and step detector). Sensor data is processed entirely on your device to count steps during a japa session. This data is not transmitted anywhere.

2. Why We Collect Data

  • App functionality: japa counting, verse browsing, quizzes, AI guidance, cloud backup, subscription management
  • Stability: crash reports help us fix bugs quickly
  • Performance: monitoring helps us keep the App fast and responsive
  • Usage insights: analytics help us understand which features are used and improve the App
  • Advertising: banner ads support the free version of the App

4. Data Retention & Deletion

  • Local data: Retained on your device until you uninstall the App or clear its data via Android Settings.
  • Cloud backup: Retained in your Google Drive until you delete it. You can delete the backup from within the App (Settings → Backup) or directly from your Google Drive.
  • Analytics & crash data: Retained per Google's standard retention policies (typically 14 months for Analytics).
  • AI chat questions: Not stored by the developer. Groq's retention policy applies to data they process.
  • Deletion requests: To request deletion of any data associated with you, contact us at gitasaathi@gmail.com. We will respond within 3 business days.

5. Children's Privacy

Gita Saathi is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal information through the App, please contact us at gitasaathi@gmail.com and we will promptly delete it.

6. Security

All network communication uses HTTPS. Subscription status is stored locally using Android's EncryptedSharedPreferences (AES-256). Cloud backups are encrypted with AES-GCM before upload. While no method of electronic transmission is 100% secure, we use commercially reasonable measures to protect your data.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top will reflect the most recent revision. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
